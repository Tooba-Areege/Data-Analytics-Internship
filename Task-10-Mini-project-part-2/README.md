## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to clean the Superstore dataset and analyze it to answer three business questions related to sales, regional performance, and discounts.

### Business Questions

1. Which product category generates the highest sales?
2. Which region contributes the highest sales and profit?
3. How do discounts affect profit?

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

The dataset was cleaned by:

- Checking the dataset structure
- Identifying missing values
- Removing duplicate records
- Converting date columns into datetime format
- Saving the cleaned dataset

After cleaning, the following analyses were performed:

- Total sales by product category
- Total sales and profit by region
- Average profit for different discount levels

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Sales were summarized for each product category.
- Regional sales and profit were compared.
- The relationship between discounts and average profit was analyzed.
