## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to build an interactive dashboard using the cleaned Superstore Sales Dataset.

The dashboard presents sales, profit, regional performance, product category performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information about customer orders, product categories, sales, profit, quantity, discounts, customer segments, and regions.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

### 1. Which product category generates the highest sales?

The dashboard compares total sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology categories to identify the highest-performing category.

### 2. Which region contributes the highest sales and profit?

The dashboard compares total sales and profit across different regions to identify the strongest regional performance.

### 3. How do discounts affect profit?

The dashboard analyzes average profit at different discount levels to understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Discount vs Average Profit
- Interactive Category Filter
- Interactive Region Filter
- Interactive Customer Segment Filter
- Filtered Dataset Preview
