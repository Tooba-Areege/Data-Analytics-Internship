## 📊 Selected Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Superstore Sales Dataset

### Dataset Description

The Superstore Sales Dataset contains information about customer orders, including product categories, sales, profit, quantity, discount, shipping mode, customer segment, and regional sales. It is commonly used for business intelligence and data analytics projects.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

### 1. Which product category generates the highest sales and profit?

This analysis will help identify the best-performing product categories and support business decisions about inventory and marketing.

### 2. Which region contributes the highest total sales and profit?

This question helps compare regional performance and identify areas with the strongest business growth.

### 3. How do discounts affect profit?

This analysis will determine whether offering larger discounts increases sales while reducing overall profitability.
